# Pathfinder — AI Education & Career Discovery Platform

Client: Upwork Client (International Educational Platform)  
Role: AI & Full Stack Engineer  
Upwork Rating: 5.0 ★★★★★  
Technologies: React, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, LLMs, LangChain, Pinecone, Figma-to-code, REST APIs

Pathfinder is an AI-powered education and career guidance platform designed for international students. Following an initial API recovery engagement on Upwork, Ariel was referred to build the complete Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system using LangChain, Pinecone vector embeddings, and OpenAI models. The platform matches students to university degree programs and viable career pathways based on their psychometric strengths, academic preferences, and financial constraints.
